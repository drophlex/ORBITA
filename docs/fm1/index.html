Connect your FM-1 and press one button: the installer finds it, writes ÓRBITA's 32 voices and checks them.

1Connect the FM-1 to this computer with a USB data cable and turn it on.
2Use Chrome or Edge. Press the button and allow MIDI.
3Wait for the check mark. Keep the cable connected while it writes (a few seconds).

Play ÓRBITA on your FM-1

  1. With ALGORITHM pick a track (T1–T4).
  2. Hold EDIT and press the 2nd white key: the track becomes FM6.
  3. Press HOME and turn KNOB 4 (SLOT) to B1 … B32: these are ÓRBITA's voices, ORSUB1 … ORTIDE4.
  4. Play the keys. To keep the sound with the track, save the project (SAVE) as usual.
  1. The FM-1 shows that it received a 32-voice bank: choose the bank (A, B, C or D) to save it in, on the FM-1.
  2. Pick a track and choose a voice of that bank: ORSUB1 … ORTIDE4.

Which FM-1 firmware?

ANDROMEDA, Dark Funk Durcal, Felucca (1.4.1 or newer): the installer talks to the FM-1, writes the voices as the FM6 voice bank (B1–B32), reads them back and checks every name. Your music, projects and presets are not touched. The built-in B voices come back with “Remove”.

Official M-VAVE firmware (V15, V16, FM1 Open): it gets the standard DX7 32-voice bank; the FM-1 asks you where to save it. That bank's previous voices are replaced: if you want to keep them, export them first with the M-VAVE Workbench. This path cannot be checked from the computer.

Only voices are written: no firmware is changed. On ANDROMEDA, Dark Funk Durcal and Felucca a cut cable leaves the old voices or the new ones, never a mix.

Nothing happens? Try another USB cable (many only charge), close other music apps that use the FM-1 (Workbench, editors, DAWs) and reload this page. Full manual: English · Español · install guide.
